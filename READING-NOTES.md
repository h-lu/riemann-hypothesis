## What the five entry points prove

In [`Main.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean), the five headline theorems share one nonvanishing result: a Dirichlet $L$-function has no zero to the right of the critical line (with the stated exception at the principal pole). The proof for primitive characters is extended to imprimitive characters by ruling out zeros of the added Euler factors in that half-plane. In the strip, the functional equation reflects a putative left-side zero to the right side for the inverse primitive character. The trivial character of modulus one gives the zeta case. See [the Euler factors](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/Imprimitive.lean) and [the strip argument](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean).

## Why a one-sided window bound suffices

The source does not need an absolute prime-error estimate for every sufficiently large argument. It uses an **upper** bound for a weighted, smoothed prime-sum error on arbitrarily far-out intervals of the form $[Y,Y^{3/2}]$. The exponent can be taken arbitrarily close to $1/2$ from above. [The window-to-nonvanishing step](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/TopWin.lean#L66) chooses the exponent after fixing a hypothetical zero.

Writing $u=\log x$, a high derivative of the relevant transform weights the error by $u^k e^{-\sigma u}/k!$. Choosing the derivative order in terms of $\log Y$ puts most of the weight on the known interval; explicit tail bounds handle its complement. The inequality uses the real-axis derivative together with the complex derivative: $|g|+g=2\max(g,0)$, so a one-sided bound on the real error $g$ is enough. Positive character weights prevent the residues of included zeros from canceling. A zero too far to the right produces a nearer pole and hence coefficient growth too rapid for the window bound. The source splits the real and nonreal pole cases. See [pole data](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/poleData.lean), [the one-sided inequality](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindowIneq.lean), and [the pole contradiction](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L83).

## Where the arithmetic estimate comes from

The exact identity in [`ChBalance.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChBalance.lean#L200) converts a prime-power count, after subtracting its main term, into a combination of smoothed character sums. The proof uses finite-character orthogonality and treats the ramified prime terms separately. [`ChTop.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChTop.lean#L37) invokes the per-unit bound and its error estimates, and [`CoreGm.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/CoreGm.lean#L12) combines them with the character estimate.

The actual route into the window construction passes through [`MainConclusion.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean), [`SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean#L69), and [`TopPlug.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/TopPlug.lean#L12). These bodies show where matching theta integrals, local matching, the covolume estimate, and the family of representation-theoretic inputs enter. The lower-level construction that combines the lift, determinant divisibility, and slope estimates is still under review. In particular, this note does not derive the suggested square-root chain length from the declarations.

## Coverage

The source links above point to the published snapshot. The proof bodies cited for the analytic implication and the main routing steps have been read. The full upstream codebase has not been read line by line. For the separate status of formal checking, see [Andrew Sutherland's verification report](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/).
